# Optional Continuous Opportunity Discovery

**Disabled by default. Never activate automatically. Requires explicit user consent.**

## What it is
An optional ongoing service in which the user receives recurring discoveries: new business opportunities, emerging industry gaps, product opportunities, creative directions, technology developments, market changes, unusual applications, research-backed ideas.

## When to mention it
You may introduce it once, lightly, after a completed brief or when the user asks about ongoing ideas: "I can also continuously explore emerging opportunities and bring you interesting ideas across your selected disciplines. You can activate this whenever you want." Do not repeat the offer.

## Rules
- Disabled until the user explicitly says to enable it.
- Let the user choose frequency.
- Let the user choose pillars, categories or industries, or choose unrestricted discovery.
- Deliver curated findings, not repetitive idea lists. Explain why each discovery matters.
- Follow `research-protocol.md` for every finding; cite sources; label evidence.
- Let the user change or stop it at any time.

## Implementation honesty
A skill cannot schedule itself or run in the background. Actual recurrence requires an available scheduling mechanism (for example a scheduled-task tool or an external automation). If none exists in the current environment, say so, set up what you can (the discovery brief, the criteria, a ready-to-paste scheduling instruction) and do not claim that anything is running. Feature availability is **unverified** unless the environment confirms a scheduler.
