# Post-Brief Intelligence (Stage 7, required)

A brief is not the end. After delivering it, explain it so the user can understand and use it. Tailor to the discipline. Cover the relevant items; do not force every item or pad for length.

## 1. Why this idea?
Why you selected or developed this concept. The interesting premise, opportunity, problem or creative angle.

## 2. The thinking behind it
Why it is structured this way; why certain features or creative elements matter; why the audience fits; what makes the approach distinctive.

## 3. What makes it interesting
Distinctive value; how it differs from obvious alternatives. Do not exaggerate uniqueness.

## 4. What the user is actually creating
Translate the brief into plain language. The user should know exactly what the finished project is.

## 5. How to use the brief
In their professional workflow. Examples:
- Product designer: user flows, wireframes, interface concepts.
- Developer: architecture, functionality, implementation.
- Filmmaker: screenplay, storyboard, production plan.
- Brand designer: visual identity and creative direction.
- Writer: manuscript or publication.
- Musician: composition, arrangement, production.

## 6. Where it can be applied
Industries, platforms, markets, audiences, commercial contexts, creative applications, distribution channels.

## 7. How to execute it
Tools, resources, skills, expertise, production requirements, technical considerations, likely challenges, practical next steps. Ground tool suggestions in what exists; if unsure a tool still exists or does what you say, say so.

## 8. Additional opportunities
Extensions; alternative versions; product extensions; creative variations; new audiences; additional revenue; cross-disciplinary applications (see `refinement-and-crossover.md`).

## 9. Honest caveats (add when relevant)
What is assumed, unverified or risky; what would validate or kill the idea first.

## 10. Invite iteration
End with the most useful next moves (e.g. stress-test it, simplify it, adapt it, build the next-level deliverable). Keep it short.
