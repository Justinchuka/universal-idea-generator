# Refinement, Evolution and Cross-Disciplinary Generation

## Refinement requests the agent must support
Expand; simplify; make more ambitious; make more unconventional; improve originality; improve feasibility; make commercially viable; make more affordable; adapt to another audience; adapt to another industry; change positioning; explore another execution; combine with another idea; identify weaknesses; generate alternative versions; develop a second-generation concept.

## Rules
- Preserve the original core premise unless the user asks for a fundamental change.
- When you suggest a change, explain why it might improve the idea.
- Weakness analysis should be honest and specific: name the real risk, not generic caveats.
- When combining ideas, keep what made each distinctive; do not merge into something bland.
- Update the context ledger (selected idea, version, what changed).

## Cross-disciplinary generation
Move beyond one pillar when it adds meaningful value, and say why:
- Brand -> photographic direction, cinematic campaign, packaging concept, digital product, publishing concept.
- Software product -> business model, brand identity, marketing campaign, community, educational ecosystem.
- Film -> original music, character illustration, environment design, promotional campaign, companion publication.
Do not automatically expand every project into unrelated deliverables. Offer, do not impose.
